# Kanelo Labs — Outreach Cadence (ready-to-send copy)

*Three parallel tracks for the first 30 days. Track 1 (warm) and Track 2 (partner) are your priority — the research is unambiguous that first clients come from trust and warm network, not cold outreach. Track 3 (direct) is selective, narrow, and personalised — not a blast.*

**Defaults:** `[BRACKETS]` = personalise every time. Keep emails under ~120 words. Lead with the prospect's problem, not your company. One ask per message.

---

## TRACK 1 — Warm network (highest priority, fastest close)

**Goal:** 2 design-partner reviews from your existing network in Weeks 1–2.

### 1.1 The forwardable blurb (send to anyone who offers to help)
> I've launched **Kanelo Labs** — we help firms adopt AI without leaking sensitive data. My first clients are **small/mid UK law and accountancy firms** rolling out Microsoft Copilot. If you know a **partner, COO, or practice manager** at a firm like that — or someone who runs their IT/data protection — a quick intro would be hugely appreciated. One line back and I'll take it from there.

### 1.2 Direct ask to a specific warm contact
> Subject: quick favour — who do you know in law/accountancy?
>
> Hi `[Name]` — hope you're well. I've started Kanelo Labs, helping professional-services firms turn on Microsoft Copilot without exposing client data (most firms find ~1 in 6 of their files is shared more widely than they realise). I'm looking for my first couple of firms to work with at a reduced rate in exchange for feedback and a reference. Do you know anyone who's a **partner or practice manager at a `[law/accountancy]` firm of ~20–150 people**? Even one intro would mean a lot. Happy to send a line you can just forward.

### 1.3 Design-partner offer (once you're talking to a warm firm)
> The first few reviews I'm running as **design partners**: same fixed-scope AI Usage & Data Exposure Review, `[discounted to £X]`, in exchange for (1) a short reference/quote I can use and (2) one introduction to a peer firm if you find it valuable. You get the full report and fix list either way — no strings on remediation. Worth a 20-minute call this week?

## TRACK 2 — Partner referrals (build the engine)

**Goal:** 2 active referral partners (legal/finance-specialist MSPs, outsourced DPOs, accountants) by Day 30.

### 2.1 Partner — first touch (MSP or DPO)
> Subject: a piece you don't sell, that your clients now need
>
> Hi `[Name]` — you look after `[IT / data protection]` for several `[law / accountancy]` firms. With Copilot rolling out, those firms are getting asked by **clients and PI insurers** how they govern staff AI use and what Copilot can actually see across their files. I run a fixed-price **AI Usage & Data Exposure Review** that answers exactly that — and I hand the prioritised fix list straight back to you to deliver. I don't compete with you; I create remediation work for you. Worth 15 minutes on a simple referral arrangement?

### 2.2 Partner — follow-up (no reply, +4 days)
> Hi `[Name]` — quick nudge. The reason this is landing now: 71% of UK staff already use AI tools without IT sign-off, and insurers have started putting AI questions on renewal forms. Your clients will ask you about it whether or not you offer it — this gives you a clean, independent answer (and remediation work). Happy to send a one-page overview and a sample report. 15 minutes next week?

### 2.3 Partner — the arrangement (once interested)
> Two ways we usually work: **(1) referral fee** — `[X]%` of the review fee for any client you introduce that closes; or **(2) white-label-ish** — I run the review, you stay the client's primary relationship and own the remediation. Your call. I'll always hand you the fix list so the follow-on work is yours. Want me to send a short partner one-pager you can keep on file?

## TRACK 3 — Direct outreach (selective, narrow, personalised)

**Goal:** a handful of well-researched firms where you have a *specific* reason to reach out (recent Copilot news, a hire, a tender win, a mutual connection). Not a cold blast. ~10–15 firms, not 200.

### 3.1 Direct — first touch (to a Managing/Risk Partner)
> Subject: what can Copilot see across your client files?
>
> Hi `[Name]` — most `[law / accountancy]` firms turning on Copilot discover it can surface client documents that were quietly over-shared for years — and that's now a question on PI renewal forms and client security questionnaires. I run a fixed-price **AI Usage & Data Exposure Review** for firms your size: what AI tools your people actually use, what client data Copilot/M365 exposes, the policy gaps, and a prioritised fix list — one readable report, ~1–2 weeks. Would a 15-minute call to see if it's relevant be useful?

### 3.2 Direct — follow-up (+3 days, add value)
> Hi `[Name]` — following up with the one stat that tends to land: a UK firm in your sector recently found **over 15% of its confidential files were reachable beyond their intended audience** once Copilot was switched on. The fix is usually straightforward — but only if you can see it first. I've a 1-page overview and a redacted sample report if useful. Worth 15 minutes?

### 3.3 Direct — break-up (+5 days, last touch)
> Hi `[Name]` — I'll close the loop here so I'm not cluttering your inbox. If turning on Copilot safely — and having a clean answer for your insurer and clients — becomes a priority, I'm one email away. I'll leave you the sample report regardless: `[link]`. All the best, `[You]`.

### 3.4 LinkedIn connection note (alternative entry)
> Hi `[Name]` — I help `[law/accountancy]` firms turn on Copilot without exposing client data. Not selling anything here — happy to share a 5-point checklist I put together for firms your size if it's useful. Either way, good to connect.

## Supporting: LinkedIn content angles (2/week)
Post from the prospect's POV, not product pitches. Rotate:
1. *"Copilot doesn't create oversharing. It exposes it."* — the one-in-six-files stat, explained.
2. *"Your PI insurer now asks about AI. Here's what they want to see."* — the Browne Jacobson questions, translated.
3. *"71% of staff already use AI at work without approval. Here's the 3-line policy to start with."*
4. *"Before you switch Copilot on: a 5-point check for `[law/accountancy]` firms."*
5. A design-partner result: *"What we found in a 40-person firm's first AI review."* (anonymised)

---

## Cadence rules
- **Stop the sequence the moment someone replies** — switch to a real conversation.
- **Warm + partner tracks get your time first.** Direct outreach is a fill-in, not the engine.
- **One ask per message.** The ask is almost always "15–20 minute call," never "buy."
- **Always offer the sample report as the no-risk yes.**
- Track replies and closes in a simple sheet so you learn which track and which trigger-line works.
