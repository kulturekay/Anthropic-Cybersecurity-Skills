# Kanelo Labs — Target-List Spec (first 50 prospects)

*The exact firmographic filters to build your first prospect list, plus where to source it and how to prioritise. Goal: a tight, high-fit list of ~50 UK law & accountancy firms you can work through warm/partner/direct in 30 days — not a 2,000-row dump.*

---

## 1. Core firmographic filters

| Filter | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Country** | United Kingdom (England & Wales first) | SRA/ICAEW/FRC + PI-insurance triggers are UK-specific |
| **Vertical** | (1) Legal services / law firms · (2) Accounting/audit/tax/advisory | The two top-ranked ICPs; sharpest pain + budget + reachability |
| **Headcount** | **15–150 staff** (sweet spot 25–100) | Big enough for budget, small enough that the partner *is* the buyer and there's no internal security gatekeeper |
| **Tech stack** | **Microsoft 365** (not Google Workspace) | The whole offer assumes M365/SharePoint/Teams/OneDrive/Copilot |
| **AI signal** | Copilot live / piloting / publicly interested in AI | Confirms the "now" trigger |
| **Security maturity** | **No in-house CISO / security team** ("CISO-light") | Where Kanelo wins; avoid firms with internal security |
| **Geography (practical)** | Within reach of your network / a city you can travel to | Warm intros and in-person readouts close faster |

**Hard excludes:** 1,000+ staff (enterprise — Darktrace/RiverSafe territory) · Google Workspace shops · firms with a named internal security function · pure public-sector bodies (procurement too slow for a 30–90-day close).

---

## 2. Buying-signal filters (prioritise rows that have these)

Score each prospect +1 per signal; work the highest scores first:
- **Hiring** an IT/operations/compliance/"innovation" role (job posts mention Copilot, M365, AI, information security).
- **Posting/commenting on LinkedIn about AI, Copilot, or AI policy** (partner or practice manager).
- **Recent Copilot/AI announcement** or "we're adopting AI" content on their site/blog.
- **Won a corporate/public tender recently** → likely facing client security questionnaires (DDQs).
- **PI renewal window** known (harder to source, but gold if a warm contact knows it).
- **Uses a generalist (non-security) MSP** → no one owns AI risk internally.
- **A mutual connection / warm path exists** (this alone jumps them to the top).

---

## 3. Buyer contacts to capture per firm

Capture 1–2 per firm (the firm is small enough that these are reachable individuals):
- **Law:** Managing Partner · Senior/Equity Partner · Practice Director / COO · Risk & Compliance Partner · IT Manager (if any).
- **Accountancy:** Managing Partner · Finance/Compliance Partner · Practice Manager / COO · IT lead.
- **For the partner track:** the firm's **MSP** and **outsourced DPO** (often listed in their privacy policy / case studies).

---

## 4. Where to source the list (cheapest → richest)

1. **Your own network + LinkedIn** — start here. 1st/2nd-degree connections at target firms = the warm track. Free and highest-converting.
2. **Law Society "Find a Solicitor" directory** (law firms, filterable by size/area) · **ICAEW "Find a Chartered Accountant"** / firm directories (accountancy).
3. **Local law societies & regional accountancy networks** — member lists; also your speaking route.
4. **Legal 500 / Chambers (smaller-firm tiers)** — credible mid/small firms, often with named partners.
5. **Companies House** — confirm size band (employee/turnover proxy via accounts) and directors.
6. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — filter by industry (Legal Practice / Accounting), headcount (11–50, 51–200), geography, and job-title; save as a lead list. Best single tool for the direct track.
7. **Apollo / similar B2B databases** — to enrich emails and confirm M365 (some show tech stack); use to fill gaps, not as the primary source.
8. **Partner referrals** — once you have 1–2 MSP/DPO partners, *their* client base is a pre-qualified list.

> **M365 confirmation tip:** MX/DNS records (Outlook/Exchange Online), "powered by Microsoft 365" footers, or simply asking on the first call. Don't over-engineer — assume M365 for UK SME professional services and confirm in conversation.

## 5. List structure (columns for your sheet)

`Firm` · `Vertical` · `City` · `Headcount band` · `M365? (Y/N/assumed)` · `AI/Copilot signal` · `Buyer name` · `Buyer title` · `Buyer LinkedIn/email` · `MSP/DPO (for partner track)` · `Warm path? (who)` · `Signal score` · `Track (warm/partner/direct)` · `Status` · `Next action/date`

---

## 6. How to work the 50 (the 30-day cut)

- **~15 "warm"** (a path exists) → Track 1. *Work first.*
- **~10 partner-sourced or MSP/DPO targets** → Track 2.
- **~25 "direct"** (no path, but high signal score) → Track 3, personalised, ~5/week.

**Quality bar:** if you can't write a *specific* first line about why you're contacting *this* firm, it doesn't belong on the direct list. Better 25 researched firms than 200 generic ones — this matches the documented lesson that founder-led first clients come from a narrow, specific, trust-led approach, not volume.

**Weekly target:** 15 warm asks (Wk2), 8 partner conversations (Wk2–3), 5 direct/week (Wk2–4), all funneling to "15-minute call → fixed-price review." Refresh signal scores weekly as firms post/hire/announce.
