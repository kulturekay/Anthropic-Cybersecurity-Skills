## SECTION 4 — How it works (de-risk)

1. **Scope & read-only access** — agreed in writing, time-boxed, least-privilege, revoked on completion.
2. **AI usage discovery** — short interviews + tenant signals map your shadow-AI footprint.
3. **Exposure scan** — read-only assessment of SharePoint / Teams / OneDrive permissions and what Copilot can reach.
4. **Gap analysis** — benchmarked against insurer, client, and ICO expectations.
5. **Report & readout** — prioritised fix list, delivered in a partner session.

> **We assess access, not content.** We look at *who can reach what* — we don't read your client files, and we don't change a thing.
